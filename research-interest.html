DCCOR Research Interest Form

Thank you for your interest in working with the Duke Center for Childhood Obesity Research (DCCOR). This form is for students, recent graduates, people taking a gap year, and anyone else interested in joining our research team. It helps us learn about your experience, availability, and goals so we can see whether there is a good fit with our current projects. Openings depend on our current projects and funding, so we are not able to offer a position to everyone who reaches out. We will review your answers and follow up with you.

Please answer each question as best you can. Some people who reach out have little or no research experience yet, and that is completely fine. Your answers are kept in this browser while you work, so you can leave and come back on the same computer.

When you finish

Click Copy my answers at the bottom of the page. Then paste your answers into an email to Karen Scherr (karen.scherr@duke.edu) from your Duke email account and attach your resume or CV as a PDF. Nothing you enter on this page is saved anywhere except in your own browser, so please send the email before you close the page.

Your answers are saved in this browser as you type. When you finish, copy them and email them to Karen Scherr (karen.scherr@duke.edu).
