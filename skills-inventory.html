DCCOR Graduate Trainee Skills Inventory

DCCOR graduate research assistants are paid to support the center's research projects while also developing invaluable real-life experience working as part of a research team. This form helps the faculty understand the skills you bring to that work. We will use your answers to assign tasks that fit your current skills and, when project needs allow, to give you chances to build the skills you are interested in. There are no right or wrong answers, and a low rating simply tells us where you may need more guidance.

For each skill, choose a rating from 0 to 4 and check the "Want to develop" box for any skill you would like to build, whether or not you have experience with it. We will keep these interests in mind when tasks come up that could help you grow in those areas. Use the Comments box on each row to tell us more about what you have actually done, such as which software or tools you use, formal training you have completed, or products related to that skill (for example, a REDCap project you built, a poster, a manuscript, or a website). If you are unsure what a skill is, rate it 0. To change a rating, click a different number, or click the selected number again to clear it. Your answers are kept in this browser while you work, so you can leave and come back on the same computer.

Rating scale

0None. No experience with this skill.
1Exposure. I have learned about it in a course or watched others do it, but I have not done it myself.
2Basic. I have done basic tasks in this area with guidance or supervision.
3Intermediate. I can handle common tasks in this area on my own and can teach others the basics. I would need help with more complex or advanced work.
4Advanced. I can handle complex tasks in this area on my own and could teach or supervise others, including on advanced aspects of the skill.

Many skills include both basic and advanced parts. Choose the rating that best describes your overall level, and use Comments to tell us which parts you know well and which you have not done.
